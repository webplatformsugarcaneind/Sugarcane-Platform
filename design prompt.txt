What it does:
CaneSetu is a full-stack sugarcane industry management platform. It digitises 
the entire sugarcane value chain — from plot registration and harvest scheduling 
to factory intake, FRP payment disbursement, and analytics. It serves four 
distinct user roles, each with their own dashboard and toolset.

User Roles:
  🌾 Farmer   — registers plots, submits cane data, tracks harvest schedules,
                 receives FRP payments, monitors market pricing
  👥 HHM      — Harvest Head Manager: coordinates harvesting gangs, assigns 
                 plots, tracks daily tonnage progress, reports to factories
  ⚒️ Labour   — finds harvest work nearby, views daily wages, claims 
                 government scheme benefits
  🏭 Factory  — manages cane supply chain, schedules crusher intake, monitors 
                 incoming tonnage, automates farmer payments, runs analytics

Key Industry Metrics:
  - FRP (Fair & Remunerative Price) in ₹ per ton
  - Payment delay in days (target: under 48 hours)
  - Contract fulfillment rate (%)
  - Profitability score (composite index)
  - Tonnage / MT (metric tonnes)
  - Crushing rate and sugar recovery rate (%)
  - Plot size in acres, cane variety, GPS coordinates
